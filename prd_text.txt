0. How to use this PRD
This file is the single source of truth. Three tracks build in parallel (section 2) and
merge by file, so every shared value lives in section 3 (Shared Contract). Section 3
beats every other section. Section 8 (battle numbers) beats section 7 (script) if they
ever disagree.
If something is unspecified: take the simplest literal reading, add one line to the
Decisions Log (section 13.4), keep going. Never change a section 3 value without
logging it.
Stub first: within 30 minutes each track commits stub files that satisfy the interfaces
in 3.7 (silent audio, rectangle sprites, hard-coded battle). Everyone integrates against
stubs, then swaps in the real thing.
Do NOT use any Nintendo or Pokemon names, sprites, music or logos. All art and
music must be original. The game borrows only the genre (8-bit monster-collecting
RPG).
1. Product
1.1 Pitch. A 3-minute pixel RPG set in an electronics store. You pick the shiny laptop, lose a
race to your rival, learn why, then win the rematch on the right AI PC.
1.2 Goal. Turn 'Why an AI PC?' into 'I want this Intel AI PC'.
1.3 Audience and setting. Gen Z walk-ins at Large Format Retail stores (Reliance Digital,
Croma, Vijay Sales) with little technical knowledge, on a touchscreen kiosk or a phone.
Hook in 5 seconds, whole run 2-3 minutes (target 180 s), plain language. Only three terms
are taught in-game: NPU, Evo, battery.
1.4 Rubric mapping.

8. Battle system (deterministic, no RNG)
8.1 Concept. A battle is a workload race, not combat. Each side has TASK (0-100, first to
100 wins), BATT (battery %), HEAT (0-100). The player picks 1 of 4 moves (tasks) per turn.
The rival follows a fixed script. Outcome is fully repeatable.

8.3 Turn rules.
1. Player acts first, then the rival (if the battle is still running).
2. prog = round(base.prog * pm * throttle) , batt = ceil(base.batt * bm) , heat
= round(base.heat * hm) . throttle = 0.7  if the actor's HEAT is 60 or more at the
start of its move, else 1. Use JS Math.round and Math.ceil.
3. Apply: TASK += prog (cap 100), BATT -= batt (floor 0), HEAT += heat (cap 100).
4. If TASK is 100: the actor COMPLETES and wins (checked before faint).
5. Else if BATT is 0: the actor FAINTS (shuts down) and the other side wins.
6. After both have acted, both HEAT = max(0, HEAT - 5). Turn counter +1.
7. At the start of BATTLE2 turn 3 emit EVENT wifi_drop , then pages W1 and W2. No
numeric effect.

unlock()  resumes the context on first gesture. Mute persists across scenes. Cross-
fade 300 ms between cues.

major third, fifth, octave) used in MUS_TITLE, MUS_WIN and MUS_RESULT (in each
cue's key), and inverted in the minor battle themes. This ties the themes together.
